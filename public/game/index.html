AFTER THE EXPERIENCE

What happens beyond the game?

Use the story to discuss concrete changes in information, conversations and campus support. Your choices are not posted anywhere.

01

Ask, then listen

“What would help in this situation?” is more useful than assuming the same support works for everyone.

02

Make options visible

Clear room information, written instructions, pause options and exit rules let people make their own choices.

03

Change the setting

Students should not carry every burden alone. Peers, staff and campus services can change how situations work.
