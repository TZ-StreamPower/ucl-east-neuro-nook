# Product requirements

Neuro Nook helps autistic and sensory-sensitive UCL East community members prepare for a campus visit, choose a route that suits their needs and share observations after a journey. The prototype journey runs from discovery and planning through navigation, reflection and future visits.

| Area | Requirement represented in the prototype |
| --- | --- |
| Explore | Let users browse places and routes by category and sensory qualities using cards or a map. Detail pages surface sensory expectations, essential information, geography and related community content. |
| Navigate | Compare Calmest and Quickest options, inspect checkpoints and sensory cues, follow a walk and find a calmer place to pause. |
| Reflect | Capture noise, lighting, crowding and how the journey felt independently. Let users save the destination rating and choose whether to share an experience. |
| Community | Surface location-linked experiences, questions and route reviews. Support saving, drafting, previewing, publishing, replying and reporting within the prototype. |
| Saved and Settings | Give users a way back to saved places, routes and posts, and controls for sound, motion, detail and route preferences. |
| Practice | Offer guided scenarios and the Same Day, Three Paths game. Make it easy to return to the originating Explore or route view. |

Explore, Navigate, Community and Saved are the four primary destinations. Place, route, Practice, Reflect and Settings support the relevant tasks.

Campus routes, travel times, environmental scores and seeded posts demonstrate the experience. Production work includes field-verified directions, credible sensory information and decisions on community privacy, moderation and persistence.
